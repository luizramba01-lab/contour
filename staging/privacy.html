4. Joining the email list (early access)

If you join our email list, we process your email address and the time you gave consent, in order to tell you about the launch of “Expedition 01” and future Contour products. The legal basis is your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), given via the checkbox in the form.

FormSubmit may process and temporarily store the data it relays and, according to publicly available information, is based outside the EU (Sri Lanka). There is no EU adequacy decision for that country, so a level of data protection comparable to the EU cannot be guaranteed. The transfer is based on your explicit consent (Art. 49(1)(a) GDPR). We receive sign-ups in an email inbox at Google (Gmail; Google Ireland Ltd., Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland); data may also be transferred to Google LLC in the USA (EU-US Data Privacy Framework).

We keep your email address until you unsubscribe or withdraw consent. You can withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future, via the unsubscribe link in our emails or by writing to [EMAIL]. This does not affect the lawfulness of processing before withdrawal. Giving your email address is voluntary, but without it we cannot keep you informed.

5. Contacting us by email

If you email us, we process the details you provide (e.g. name, email address, message) to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR) and delete them once it is resolved, unless statutory retention duties apply.

6. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18) and data portability (Art. 20), and the right to withdraw consent at any time with effect for the future (Art. 7(3) GDPR).

Right to object: where we process data on the basis of Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR, you may object at any time on grounds relating to your particular situation (Art. 21 GDPR).

Right to complain: you may lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), e.g. the Berlin Commissioner for Data Protection and Freedom of Information, Alt-Moabit 59–61, 10555 Berlin, Germany.

7. Security and automated decisions

The website is delivered over encrypted HTTPS only. No automated decision-making, including profiling, takes place.

8. Changes

We will update this policy whenever the website or the services we use change (for example when an online shop opens). The version published here applies.

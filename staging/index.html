04 / Field notesDesigned in Europe

Drawn from survey maps.

Every graphic starts as a real contour line.

We design in Europe, tracing ridgelines and river bends off old survey sheets, then produce in small, considered runs — enough to wear hard, never enough to waste.

05 / Origin45.92°N 7.62°E → 52.52°N 13.40°E

From 2,050 m
to 34 m.

CONTOUR is named after the line on a map that joins every point at the same height. This is the story of how we lost our mountains and went looking for new ones.

  1. 2,050M
    01 · Under the Matterhorn

    It started in the Alps above Cervinia, where every run goes up and every map is mostly contour lines.

  2. 34M
    02 · Then, Berlin

    A flat, fast, beautiful city, with no mountain in sight. The runs got longer. The map went blank.

  3. 120M
    03 · Every city has a summit

    So we went looking, and found them everywhere: rubble hills, bridges, stairwells, the last ramp of a car park at 6 am.

Every graphic we print starts as a real contour line, so you carry the mountain with you, even when the city is flat.

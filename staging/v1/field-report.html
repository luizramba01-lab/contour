Test 01 — Wash · LavaggioCampioni in arrivo

Wash.

Does the sublimated print hold its colour, and does the knit keep its shape, after repeated washing?

Method
Domestic machine wash at 30 °C, inside out, no softener, line dry. Photographed against a reference swatch every 5 cycles.
Measure
Print colour against the reference, chest and length in cm before / after, pilling on the underarm and side panels.
Target
30 wash cycles per colourway.

Test 02 — Wear · UsoCampioni in arrivo

Wear.

What happens to seams, thumb openings and the watch slit after real miles?

Method
Worn on logged runs in Berlin (flat, cold starts) and on climbs. Each run logged with distance and temperature.
Measure
Chafing points, flatlock seam wear, thumb-opening stretch, whether the watch slit stays flat and in place.
Target
At least 200 km per sample.

Test 03 — Sweat · SudoreCampioni in arrivo

Sweat.

How fast does the knit move moisture and dry, and how does it smell after a long run?

Method
Long runs and interval sessions, weighed before and after; drying time measured indoors at room temperature.
Measure
Grams of sweat held at the finish, minutes to dry, odour after 24 h unwashed, comfort of the mesh panels.
Target
Repeated on every colourway, including the dark CW02 and CW03 bases.

Test 04 — Reflective · RiflettenteCampioni in arrivo

Reflective.

Can drivers see the reflective tab and printed details at night, and does it survive the wash test?

Method
Photographed in car headlights on a dark road at fixed distances, new and after the wash cycles of test 01.
Measure
Distance at which the reflective tab is clearly visible, cracking or peeling of the transfer.
Target
New sample and after 30 washes.
